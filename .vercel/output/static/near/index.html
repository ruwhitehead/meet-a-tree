Where to look

Trees are not scattered at random. Pick the place you’re standing in and you can usually narrow it to three or four candidates before you even look at a leaf.

A street or town park

Planted for shade and toughness. Look up at the crown shape first — street trees are usually pruned, so leaves and bark tell you more than outline.

  • Horse chestnut
  • Sycamore
  • Silver birch
  • Rowan

An old churchyard

The most reliable place in lowland Britain to meet a genuinely ancient tree. Check the girth: anything over 5 m round is likely centuries old.

  • Yew
  • Holly
  • Ash

A hedgerow or field edge

Hedges were planted to be stock-proof, so expect thorns. Count woody species along thirty paces — roughly one per century of hedge age.

  • Hawthorn
  • Elder
  • Ash
  • English oak

Chalk or limestone downland

Thin alkaline soil suits a particular set of trees. Beech hangers on scarp slopes are the signature of the southern chalk.

  • Beech
  • Yew
  • Hawthorn

Heath, sandy or acid ground

Poor, free-draining soil favours pioneers and conifers. Birch arrives first; pine follows and stays.

  • Silver birch
  • Scots pine
  • Rowan

Ancient or damp woodland

Look at the ground flora as well as the canopy — bluebells, dog’s mercury and ramsons all say the wood is old.

  • English oak
  • Beech
  • Ash
  • Holly

Uplands, crags and mountainsides

Trees survive here where grazing animals cannot reach. A lone tree wedged in a crag is almost always rowan.

  • Rowan
  • Silver birch
  • Scots pine

Does this know where I am?

No — and that is deliberate. This page asks for no location permission and sends nothing anywhere. It works the same on any phone, in any country, with no signal, because it is habitat guidance rather than a map of your surroundings.

When live species counts arrive, they will work like this: tap a button, your phone asks you whether to share your location, and only then do we query open GBIF and iNaturalist records for that rough area — rounded to about a kilometre, never your exact position, and never stored. If you decline, or your phone can’t get a fix, or you are offline, you keep this page. Location will always be the optional extra, never the way in.
