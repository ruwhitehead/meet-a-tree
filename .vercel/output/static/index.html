Summer · what to look for

English oak — Acorns swell green on their stalks. Look for spangle galls on the leaf undersides and marble galls on twigs — both are wasp nurseries, not disease.

Hawthorn — Green haws form; hedges hum with insects. Hawthorn shield bugs and countless moth larvae feed on the leaves.
